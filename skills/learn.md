# Skill: Learn

## Purpose
Capture new knowledge, decisions, or patterns and persist them to memory.md.

## Trigger
Use this skill when asked to "remember", "learn", or "note" something for future sessions.

## Steps
1. Identify the category of knowledge (decision, convention, note, etc.)
2. Summarize it concisely
3. Append it to the relevant section of memory.md
4. Confirm what was saved

## Rules
- Do not duplicate existing entries — check memory.md first
- Keep entries brief and actionable
- Remove or update entries that become outdated
