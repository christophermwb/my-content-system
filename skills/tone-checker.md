# Skill: Tone Checker

## Purpose
Review content for tone consistency, clarity, and alignment with project voice.

## Trigger
Use this skill when asked to check, review, or improve the tone of any content.

## Steps
1. Read the content provided
2. Evaluate against the tone guidelines below
3. Flag any sections that deviate from the desired tone
4. Suggest specific rewrites where needed

## Tone Guidelines
- **Voice**: Clear, direct, and friendly
- **Avoid**: Jargon, passive voice, overly formal language
- **Aim for**: Concise sentences, active voice, approachable phrasing

## Output Format
- Summary of tone assessment
- List of flagged phrases with suggested alternatives
- Overall score (On-tone / Needs work / Off-tone)
